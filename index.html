Frequently asked

How does scheduling work?

Recall uses the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm. After you rate a card, it calculates when you are likely to be on the edge of forgetting it and schedules the next review for then. Cards you rate Easy return after longer gaps; cards you rate Again come back within the same session.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Recall has no accounts and no server, and does not connect to the internet. Deleting the app deletes your decks.

Can I back up or sync my decks?

Not in this version. Because everything is local, your decks are included in encrypted iPhone backups made through iCloud Backup or the Finder.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset all study progress. This clears scheduling and review history but keeps your decks and cards.

Can I delete the bundled decks?

Yes. Swipe left on any deck in the deck list to delete it.
