Privacy Policy

Recall — last updated 26 August 2026

Summary

Recall does not collect any data.

What we collect

Nothing. Recall has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications.

Your content

The decks and cards you create are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. They are never transmitted anywhere. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Network access

Recall does not connect to the internet. It contains no networking code.

Children

Recall is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
